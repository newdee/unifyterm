# Surface: the product page (site/index.html)

Scope: the one public page for UnifyTerm, at dfine.tech/unifyterm/. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience and job: a developer who works on two or three of Windows, macOS and Linux decides in a couple of minutes whether to install. Action: copy the install command for their system. Proof: real pictures of the program, measured numbers, the limits said plainly. Languages: English first, Chinese one click away. Copy voice: plain, as a person says it.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One document, valid at three borders. The page is a travel document, not a terminal demo: it refuses the dark neon hero with a fake terminal typing by itself, and the three icon cards under it.

OWN-WORLD: Pale security paper that shifts tint down the page, with fine guilloche line work in the logo's three inks (blue, purple, cyan); deep navy text; rubber stamps, each in one ink, slightly turned, carrying a measured fact; field labels with their values; a machine-readable band in a cockpit mono. Titling capitals of an engraved face for names of things. Dark theme: the navy cover with gold foil.

STORY: The visitor sees that the same commands work on all three systems, sees the real program do it, reads what is not done yet, and takes the command for their system.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The data page of the document. Left, the real terminal picture mounted like the holder's photograph, a stamp overlapping its corner. Right, the name in titling capitals, the sentence "One terminal. Valid on Windows, macOS and Linux.", and four fields with values. Under both, three entry stamps (WINDOWS, MACOS, LINUX). Across the bottom, the machine-readable band: the install command for the visitor's system, with tabs for the others and a copy control; this band is the primary action.

FORM: A passport (candidate 7 of 7 on the ordered list; seed key 3a3cf15f; the roll ran degraded, with no challengers). Signature interaction: copying the command stamps "COPIED" beside it. Motion grammar: a stamp lands once when its page comes into view (pressed down, a little ink spread), and nothing else moves.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
